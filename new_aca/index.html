Common questions

Is this an official government website?

No. This is an independent educational page. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the U.S. government or HealthCare.gov. You can apply directly through the official Marketplace.

Will I qualify for lower monthly premiums?

Possibly. Eligibility and the amount of savings depend on your household and other requirements. A Marketplace application provides your actual eligibility results and plan prices. No savings or premium amount is guaranteed. Learn about Marketplace savings.

Can I enroll at any time?

Generally, you can enroll during Open Enrollment. Outside that period, you may qualify for a Special Enrollment Period based on your circumstances. Dates and rules may differ by state Marketplace. Check official enrollment information.

Does this form enroll me or request a callback?

No. This form is a demonstration only. It does not submit information, request contact, verify eligibility, or enroll anyone in a health plan.
